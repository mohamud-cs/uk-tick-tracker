    Species guide

    Ixodes ricinus (castor bean tick)

    The most common tick in the UK and the main carrier of Lyme disease. Found in woodland, moorland, parks and gardens.

    Ixodes hexagonus (hedgehog tick)

    Often found on hedgehogs, but also feeds on dogs, cats and foxes. Common in gardens.

    Dermacentor reticulatus (ornate dog tick)

    Has a marbled pattern on its back. Found in parts of western Britain and Essex. Can carry the parasite that causes babesiosis in dogs.

    Prevention tips

    • Wear long trousers and tuck them into your socks.
    • Use a repellent that works on ticks.
    • Stay on paths and avoid long grass.
    • Check yourself, children and pets after being outdoors.
    • Remove a tick with fine tipped tweezers, pulling steadily and close to the skin.
    • See a GP if you get a rash or feel unwell after a tick bite.
